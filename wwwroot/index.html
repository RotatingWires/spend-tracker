About SpendTracker

A self-hosted spending tracker. Every statistic comes from entries you recorded; SpendTracker never guesses categories or fills in data for you.

Purchase: money you spent. For who says who it was for. Pick several people to split it; each friend's share counts toward what they owe you, unless you check Gift for them. Gifts stay off their balance unless People → Include gifts is checked.

Refund / return: money a store gave back. It is linked to the original purchase and one person's share of it, uses that purchase's tags and collections, and reduces net spent on the date it was refunded.

Collections work like tags: add them on the Collections tab (a trip, a move) and choose them on purchases. The Collections tab and Stats total each collection's purchases and their refunds.

Time frame (toolbar) chooses what the ledger and calendar show: All time, one month, or several months checked together. ◀ / ▶ move the chosen months by one.

Payback to me: money someone else gave you for something you paid for. Link it to a purchase to settle their share of it, or leave it unlinked to reduce their overall balance. Paybacks do not change net spent; Stats shows them separately.

Net spent is purchases minus refunds in the selected period. Average per day divides by every calendar day in the period up to today; average per spending day divides only by days with at least one purchase.

Backups (File → Download Backup) are ZIP files holding a copy of the database: every entry, setting, and the full history, but never your password. Restoring replaces all current data after saving a recovery copy on the server.

Archiving a payment method, person, tag, collection, or merchant on its tab only stops it being offered for new entries; it stays on existing transactions and in search and Stats. Archiving a transaction hides it from the ledger and calendar (View → Show Archived lists it again); it still counts in Stats, People balances, and refund limits.

IDs: every entry has a permanent ID (#12) that is never reused; File → Search... finds #12, or any merchant, description, note, tag, collection, or amount, in every transaction. Each entry is also numbered inside every person, tag, collection, merchant, and payment method it belongs to, such as Alex #3 or food #12, shown at the top of the editor. Select a number there, or a row on the People, Payment Methods, Tags, Collections, or Merchants tab, to see its whole numbered list.

File → Select... picks several entries in the ledger (Shift-click selects a range; Escape or Select... again stops) to archive, tag, add to a collection, split with people, set the merchant of, or delete them together. Entries an action cannot apply to are skipped and listed with the reason.

Amounts are shown in US dollars.
