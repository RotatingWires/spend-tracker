About SpendTracker

A self-hosted spending tracker. Every statistic comes from entries you recorded; SpendTracker never guesses categories or fills in data for you.

Purchase: money you spent. For who says who it was for. Pick several people to split it; each friend's share counts toward what they owe you.

Refund / return: money a store gave back. It is linked to the original purchase and one person's share of it, uses that purchase's tags, and reduces net spent on the date it was refunded.

Payback to me: money someone else gave you for something you paid for. Link it to a purchase to settle their share of it, or leave it unlinked to reduce their overall balance. Paybacks do not change net spent; Stats shows them separately.

Net spent is purchases minus refunds in the selected period. Average per day divides by every calendar day in the period up to today; average per spending day divides only by days with at least one purchase.

Backups (File → Download Backup) are ZIP files holding a copy of the database: every entry, setting, and the full history, but never your password. Restoring replaces all current data after saving a recovery copy on the server.

Archiving a payment method, person, tag, or merchant under Manage only stops it being offered for new entries; it stays on existing transactions and in search and Stats. Archiving a transaction hides it from the ledger and calendar (View → Show Archived lists it again); it still counts in Stats, People balances, and refund limits.

IDs: every entry has a permanent ID (#12) that is never reused; search #12 to find it. Each entry is also numbered inside every person, tag, merchant, and payment method it belongs to, such as Alex #3 or food #12, shown at the top of the editor. Select a number there, or a payment method, person, tag, or merchant under Manage, to see its whole numbered list.

File → Select... picks several entries in the ledger (Shift-click selects a range; Escape or Select... again stops) to archive, tag, split with people, set the merchant of, or delete them together. Entries an action cannot apply to are skipped and listed with the reason.

Amounts are shown in US dollars.
