About SpendTracker

A self-hosted spending tracker. Every statistic comes from entries you recorded; SpendTracker never guesses categories or fills in data for you.

Purchase: money you spent. For who says who it was for; a purchase for someone else counts toward what they owe you.

Refund / return: money a store gave back. It is linked to the original purchase, uses that purchase's person and tags, and reduces net spent on the date it was refunded.

Payback to me: money someone else gave you for something you paid for. Link it to a purchase to settle that purchase, or leave it unlinked to reduce their overall balance. Paybacks do not change net spent; Stats shows them separately.

Net spent is purchases minus refunds in the selected period. Average per day divides by every calendar day in the period up to today; average per spending day divides only by days with at least one purchase.

Backups (File → Download Backup) contain every entry, setting, and the full history, but never your password. Restoring replaces all current data after saving a recovery copy on the server.

Amounts are shown in US dollars.
